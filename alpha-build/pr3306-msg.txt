feat: bring Find My closer to the iPhone app

Map framing, selection, and drawer layout now follow the iPhone Find My app.

- Cache each friend's/device's last known location (per server + iCloud account)
  so the map still shows them when Apple returns no current fix.
- Frame the map per tab: People = your location plus the nearby cluster;
  Devices/Items = the cluster holding most products (your location ignored).
- Group overlapping device/item markers into count capsules below street zoom;
  tapping a capsule zooms in and splits it. Friends are never grouped.
- One selection for map and list: tapping a marker or a row zooms to it,
  raises its marker, and expands the row into an inset card (name, distance,
  street, status) with Directions and, for saved contacts, Contact.
  The card is scrolled into view after the expand animation settles.
- Apple-style row text: distance (Nearby / 2.4 mi / 1,671 mi, km outside
  US/UK) and "place · N min. ago".
- Devices fall back to Apple's crowdSourcedLocation when location is empty
  (e.g. Wi-Fi iPads), preferring whichever fix is newer.
- AirPods are listed under Devices; Items holds AirTags and other accessories.
- Favorites (server favorite_order) are listed first in the Friends tab.
- Contact lookup falls back to the raw Find My address when the handle is not
  in the local database; display names are never treated as addresses.
